# Guest Notice (English)

**Who is processing your data?**

Your host ([Host Name]) is the data controller for your personal data. Ostra is a service that processes your data on behalf of your host to provide guest communication and booking management.

**Why is your phone number used?**

Your phone number is used to:
- Send you automated check-in, check-out, and review request messages
- Allow the host to communicate with you via WhatsApp
- Provide an AI assistant that can answer your questions about the apartment

**AI Assistant**

Some messages you receive may be generated by an AI assistant that answers questions based on information about the apartment. The AI does not have access to other guests' data or personal information.

**How long is your data kept?**

Your personal data (name, phone number, messages) is kept for [90 days, configurable by host] after your check-out date. Booking dates and apartment details are kept indefinitely for occupancy history.

**Your Rights**

You have the right to:
- Access a copy of your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your personal data
- Object to processing (by replying "STOP" to any message)
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact your host directly.

**Opt-out**

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying "STOP" to any message. This will stop all future messages for your phone number with this host.

**Contact**

For questions about your data, contact your host at [Host Email].

---

**Note:** This notice should be translated into the languages of your guests and displayed or sent to them as appropriate. Adjust the bracketed values ([Host Name], [90 days], [Host Email]) to match your specific configuration.
